SABSE IMPORTANT LINE
"System umpire ko assist karta hai, replace nahi karta. Video evidence deta hai, lekin final decision hamesha insaan (umpire) ka hota hai."

1 Project kya hai

  • Tape-ball cricket ke umpires ki madad ke liye mobile app.
  • 4 problems solve karta hai: umpiring disputes, manual scoring errors, video evidence ki kami, digital records na hona.
  • Version 1 mein koi AI / Snicko / Hawk-Eye / Ultra Edge nahi — koi automatic decision nahi, sirf assistance.

2 Architecture

Mobile App (Flutter/Dart) → Local Database (SQLite) → Firebase Backend (Auth + Firestore) → Cloud Storage
  • Offline-first: ground par internet na ho tab bhi scoring chalti rehti hai.
  • Internet wapas aaye to data automatically cloud se sync ho jata hai.
FlutterDartFirebase Auth FirestoreSQLiteCamera API

3 Data model — 9 entities

User, Tournament, Match (center/hub), Team, Player, Innings, Ball Event, Video, Review, Statistics.

Har Ball Event aur Video ek unique Match ID se linked hota hai (BR5, BR6, BR9).

4 Non-functional points

  • Performance: score turant update, offline mein bhi local data accessible.
  • Safety: camera permission, storage warning, undo/correction facility.
  • Security: role-based access, secure login, protected videos.
  • Constraint: system kabhi guarantee nahi deta ke decision "sahi" hai — responsibility umpire ki hai.

Q: AI kyun use nahi kiya? ▶
A: Version 1 ka scope jaan-bujh kar simple rakha gaya hai. AI, Snicko, Hawk-Eye jaisi cheezein future enhancement ke liye chhodi gayi hain — abhi ka focus reliable scoring aur video-assisted review hai.
Q: Internet na ho to kya hoga? ▶
A: Local SQLite database sab kuch offline store karta hai — scoring, match data. Jab internet wapas aata hai, sab kuch automatically Firebase cloud se sync ho jata hai (BR10).
Q: Scoring mein mistake ho jaye to? ▶
A: Undo/correction facility hai (FR-4.11). Har correction record hoti hai (BR8), taake original ball event ka trace bhi maujood rahe.
Q: Data security kaise ensure hoti hai? ▶
A: Role-based access control, HTTPS communication, protected credentials aur secure user sessions. Har role sirf apne functions tak access rakhta hai.
Q: System khud decide kyun nahi karta ke out hai ya nahi? ▶
A: Ye project ka core principle hai (Chapter 1, FR7, Business Rule BR3/BR4) — system sirf video evidence aur slow-motion replay deta hai, final decision hamesha human umpire ka hota hai. Isse trust aur accountability barqarar rehti hai.
Q: Video recording ki privacy kaise handle hoti hai? ▶
A: SRS section 5.5 ke mutabiq — players/officials ki privacy respect ki jati hai, zaroorat parne par consent li jati hai, aur app clearly batati hai ke video sirf review/assistance tool hai.
Q: Future mein kya add ho sakta hai? ▶
A: Automatic AI umpiring, ball tracking, Snicko/UltraEdge, Hawk-Eye analysis, automatic LBW/catch detection, aur professional multi-camera broadcasting — ye sab SRS mein future enhancements ke taur par likhe hain, is version ka commitment nahi.
